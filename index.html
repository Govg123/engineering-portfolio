PURDUE SPACE PROGRAM

Autonomous Rocket Gantry & Landing Interface

Mechanical design for a gantry system supporting an autonomously launching and landing rocket, with emphasis on interfaces, assembly, manufacturability, and cross-team integration.

My contribution

Designed and iterated structural components in Siemens NX and worked on the mechanical interface between the rocket and landing system. Design decisions had to account for loading, stiffness, assembly, manufacturability, and interfaces with controls.

Engineering takeaway

A component can be correct in CAD and still fail at the system level. Interface requirements, assembly access, sensing, controls, and downstream hardware all matter.

Tools

Siemens NX · MATLAB · mechanical design · structures · integration · design reviews

PURDUE COOLING TECHNOLOGIES RESEARCH CENTER

TPMS Heat Exchanger CFD & Validation

Computational design and analysis of advanced heat exchanger geometries for high-density data-center cooling, with a focus on heat transfer, pressure drop, flow behavior, and model validation.

What I analyze

Temperature distributions, velocity fields, pressure drop, and overall thermal performance in liquid-to-liquid heat exchanger configurations.

How I validate

I compare numerical results against heat-transfer and fluid-mechanics fundamentals and investigate mesh quality, boundary conditions, material properties, and modeling assumptions when results do not make physical sense.

Tools

OpenFOAM · ANSYS · CFD · heat transfer · fluid mechanics · post-processing

Presented co-authored work on functionally graded TPMS heat exchangers at Purdue’s Summer Undergraduate Research Conference.

PURDUE SOLAR CAR RACING

Brake System Design & Vehicle Integration

Mechanical design work on the primary braking system, balancing driver effort, packaging, safety, manufacturability, and integration with the vehicle.

Problem

Initial calculations showed the braking system would require roughly 200 lb of driver pedal force—far too high for practical operation.

Iteration

Reworked the braking-system geometry to increase mechanical advantage while considering packaging, structural integrity, safety, and manufacturability.

Result

Reduced the calculated pedal-force requirement to approximately 40 lb.
